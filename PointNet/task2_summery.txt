For the classification, the original PointNet has higher accuracy than the modified PointNet, even though the modified model has a lower test loss.
This is most likely because the original PointNet uses max pooling, which focuses on the most distinctive features of the shape.
The modified PointNet, with average pooling, spreads attention across all points, which can reduce the impact of strong discriminative features.
This results in slightly lower accuracy, even though the predictions are less extreme and therefore produce a lower loss.
For segmentation, the modified PointNet performs better, achieving both higher accuracy and lower loss compared to the original model.
We think this is because segmentation depends much more on local features rather than global shape information.
The changes, such as average pooling, additional hidden layer, and the use of LeakyReLU, help preserve information from all points.
